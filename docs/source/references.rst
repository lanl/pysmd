References
==========

The references below focus on extended-Lagrangian Born--Oppenheimer molecular
dynamics (XL-BOMD), shadow Born--Oppenheimer molecular dynamics, and the
density-matrix, charge-relaxation, and computational methods that directly
support them. Papers are grouped by their primary contribution rather than by
publication date. The list is a selected technical bibliography rather than
an exhaustive survey of the field.

Scope note
----------

This page emphasizes peer-reviewed work directly relevant to XL-BOMD and
shadow dynamics. Papers are included when they provide foundational theory,
algorithms, implementation methods, or closely related applications. The list
is selective; preprints and papers without a direct connection to these topics
are generally omitted.
